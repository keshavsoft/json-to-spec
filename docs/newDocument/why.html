Why separate structure from data?

The project keeps the UI blueprint and business payload independent, then merges them through a deterministic compiler.

Blueprint stays stable

Structure describes how a screen should look without embedding the business payload.

Data stays reusable

Data can change without rewriting the visual structure.

Compiler owns the merge

Resolve values, replace placeholders and expand iterations in one pipeline.
